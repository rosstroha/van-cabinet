# Component candidates

Review the cabinet layout and approve components before buying or cutting. Current concept: 30 W x 18 D x 33 H inches; clear bays are 12.5 inches left and 16 inches right. Depth remains provisional. Dog weight is 45 lb; seated person weight is 200 lb. These are load targets, not validated ratings.

| Candidate | Manufacturer dimensions | Fit and service notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thetford Porta Potti 335 | 13.46 W x 15.03 D in; published height differs between regional pages | Model reserves 13.5 in height. 2.6 gal waste and flush tanks. Confirm actual height, hold-down and operating clearances. |
| COMET S8351.82.31-19 | 475 x 280 x 170 mm; 19 L | Model uses 11.024 W x 6.693 D x 18.701 H in. Confirm upright use and port orientation before purchase. |
| Dometic VA930 | 280 W x 380 D x 145 H mm | Left-side sink candidate. Published cutout 272 x 372 mm; obtain exact template. Faucet and drain layout pending. |
| Dometic 972 / 976 | 13.125 W x 15.25 D in; heights 12.5 / 15.25 in, tolerance +/-0.375 in | Alternate toilets with 2.6 / 5 gal waste capacity. 976 needs separate height review. |
| Fiamma Roll-Tank 23W | 500 x 330 x 250 mm; 22.5 L | Manufacturer prohibits travel full and upright. Flat orientation conflicts with current bay. |
| Class A Customs T-0500 | 17.5 x 7 x 9.5 in +/-0.25, excluding fittings; 5 gal | Rotatable RV greywater tank. Needs vent, carrier and accessible disconnects. |

Use a floor-supported toilet sled with parking restraint, toilet hold-down and travel latch. The load includes the seated person, toilet contents and tray. Confirm the floor is level through the full wheel path. The door opens independently so it does not obstruct seated use.

Accuride 9308E is a provisional dog-platform slide candidate: 12 in length, lock-in/lock-out, full extension, 3 in tall and 0.75 in side clearance each. Its mobile rating applies under manufacturer test conditions. It does not establish the platform capacity; jumping loads and fastener attachment require validation. Its data sheet prohibits supporting human weight. Do not use it as the seated toilet support.

The COMET has a DIN96 side cleanout, DIN61 top connection and a supplied 1 m hose for a 28 mm ID connection. Reserve access for vent and inlet disconnects, a sink odor trap, and tank removal. Retain the tank during transport. Five US gallons of water weigh approximately 42 lb before tank weight. Weekend or week-long capacity depends on occupants and sink/flush use.

Use rigid mounting spacers across the 0.875 in rear gap. Locate van structure and concealed services before selecting either rivet nuts with 1/4-20 bolts and washers, or stainless self-drilling pan-head screws with washers. Anchor locations, plywood joints, hinges, latches, roller hardware and platform structure remain unfinished.
